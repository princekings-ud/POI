About the Candidate

The Man Behind
the Vision

"Every community in Edo South deserves a voice that is heard — and a representative with the strength to act on that voice."

Pastor (Dr.) Osagie Ize-Iyamu is one of Nigeria's most recognisable political figures from Edo State — a man of deep faith, proven leadership, and an unrelenting commitment to the development of his people. With over three decades of service spanning public governance and community development, he brings unmatched depth of experience to the Senate race.

Born and raised in Edo State, Pastor Ize-Iyamu has dedicated his life to the service of ordinary Nigerians — championing education, economic empowerment, healthcare access, and infrastructure development. His Senate campaign represents the next chapter in a life already given to public service.

Our Commitments

What We Will
Deliver for Edo

Specific, measurable commitments — not vague promises. These are the legislative priorities that will define a first term in the Senate.

Security & Social Welfare

Advocate for community policing funding, emergency response infrastructure, social welfare offices in all three senatorial districts, and legislation to protect widows, pensioners, and vulnerable persons in Edo South.

Infrastructure & Urban Renewal

Secure federal allocation to rehabilitate major roads, construct bridges, deploy drainage systems to combat flooding, and connect every town and village in Edo South to the national electricity grid.

Manpower & Education

Sponsor legislation for free and compulsory education, establish Technical and Vocational Training Centres in each LGA, provide ICT skills programmes, and fund scholarships for Edo South students in higher institutions.

Public Private Partnership

Legislate for Technology Incubation Centres and Export Processing Zones in Edo South, attract inward investment through an International Edo Development Summit, and establish industrial parks to drive manufacturing and job creation.

Healthcare for All

Fund Primary Health Care Centres across all 192 wards, upgrade referral hospitals, deploy mobile health service buses to rural communities, and establish a Disease Control Centre to tackle HIV, TB, and malaria in Edo South.

Employment & Youth Empowerment

Establish an Edo Employment Bureau, create entrepreneurship platforms with access to funding across all 18 LGAs, reintroduce school sports, and ensure every school leaver acquires a certified vocational skill before graduation.
